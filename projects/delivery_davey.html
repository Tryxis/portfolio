Delivery Davey

Small driving and delivery prototype

Zoom around a neighbourhood picking up and delivering packages as quickly as possible. Picking up packages generates speed, crashing loses it — so drive carefully!

Game
Play Me View Repo

Build notes

The risk/reward loop is the whole game

Speed is the score and the hazard at once. Collecting a package boosts you; clipping the scenery drops you back down. One tuned number in each direction is enough to make a player decide whether that shortcut is worth it, which is more interesting than a timer.

Kept deliberately small

Three scripts: one drives the car, one handles pickup and drop-off triggers, one follows the camera. Every tuning value is exposed in the Unity inspector, so balancing the thing is done by playing it rather than by recompiling.
