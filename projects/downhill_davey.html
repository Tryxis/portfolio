Downhill Davey

Small platform prototype

Flip and trick your way down Mount Muckle. Bonking your head will send you back to the top.

Game
Play Me View Repo

Build notes

Physics does the animating

The rider is a 2D rigidbody. Steering applies torque rather than setting a rotation, so landings, wipeouts and the satisfying wobble at the top of a jump all fall out of the physics rather than being hand-animated.

A crash rule you can feel

A separate collider on the head turns a bad landing into an instant restart. Making the failure condition a body part rather than a health bar means players learn it in one go.
